**Goal:** Implement the core business logic necessary to convert free users into paying subscribers at scale.
**Depends on:** Phase 5
**Plans:** 2 plans

Plans:

- [x] 06-01: Implement test-date onboarding flow and upgrade moment triggers
- [x] 06-02: Implement tiered pricing and upgrade modal

**Details:**

### Phase 7: Growth & Retention

**Goal:** Deploy features designed to increase top-of-funnel acquisition and re-engage dormant users.
**Depends on:** Phase 6
**Plans:** 2 plans

Plans:

- [x] 07-01: Implement i18n expansion and "Help a Mate" referral program
- [x] 07-02: Implement email notification system
